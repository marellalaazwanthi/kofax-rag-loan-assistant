
Loan approval policy:

1. Minimum credit score required for unsecured personal loan is 650.
2. Annual income must be at least 1.5 times the requested loan amount.
3. Applicants with contract employment require additional verification.
4. Missing income proof or identity proof results in manual review.
5. Applications that fail both credit score and income checks are rejected automatically.
6. Required documents include:
   - loan_application_form
   - drivers_license
   - income_proof
7. For loan amounts above 25000, bank_statement is also required.
8. Manual review may be triggered for borderline credit scores between 620 and 649.
9. Missing employer verification for contract workers triggers manual review.
